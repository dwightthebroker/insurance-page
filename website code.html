How much life insurance do I need?

A common starting point is 10 to 12 times your annual income. From there, adjust for your mortgage, other debts, and how many years your family would need support, including college costs for kids.

Is the life insurance from my employer enough?

Usually not. Employer coverage is often a small multiple of salary, and it typically ends when you leave the job. A policy you own stays with you.

When do I sign up for Medicare?

Your Initial Enrollment Period runs seven months: the three months before the month you turn 65, your birthday month, and the three months after. Miss it and you may face lifelong late penalties on some parts. Each year, the Annual Enrollment Period runs October 15 to December 7.

What is an annuity and how does it work?

An annuity is a contract with an insurance company. You put in money, either all at once or over time, and it can pay you income later, sometimes for the rest of your life. Common types are fixed, fixed indexed, and variable, and they fit different goals.

Does it cost more to use an insurance broker?

Generally no. Carriers pay brokers through commission built into the product, so you typically pay the same premium whether you buy direct or through a broker. The difference is that an independent broker can compare several companies for you.
